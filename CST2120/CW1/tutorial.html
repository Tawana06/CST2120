Welcome to Arctic Maze Dash!

Your goal is simple: Race the Snowman (⛄) to the flag (🚩).

Controls:

  • Use the Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) to move your penguin (🐧).
  • The computer Snowman (⛄) will move one step every time you move.

The Rules:

  • If you reach the flag first, you win the level and move on to a bigger maze!
  • If the computer reaches the flag first, you lose and have to restart.
  • You cannot walk through the dark blue walls.
